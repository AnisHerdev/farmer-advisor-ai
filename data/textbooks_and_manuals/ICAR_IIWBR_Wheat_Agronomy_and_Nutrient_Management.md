## 1. SOWING DYNAMICS & SEED MANAGEMENT

### 1.1 Sowing Timelines in North-Western & North-Eastern Plains
1. **Timely Sown Irrigated Condition:**
   - Optimal Window: November 1 to November 20.
   - Recommended Varieties: HD-2967, PBW-550, DBW-187 (Karan Vandana), DBW-222 (Karan Narendra), HD-3086 (Pusa Gautami).
   - Expected Yield: 55 to 65 quintals per hectare.
2. **Late Sown Irrigated Condition:**
   - Permissible Window: November 25 to December 25.
   - Varieties: WR-544 (Pusa Gold), PBW-373, HD-3118, DBW-107.
   - Note on Terminal Heat Stress: Every day's delay beyond November 20 reduces potential yield by 35 to 40 kg/ha due to high temperatures (>32°C) during grain filling in March causing forced maturity and shriveled grains.

## 2. WATER MANAGEMENT & CRITICAL GROWTH STAGES

### 2.1 Critical Phenological Stages for Irrigation (Order of Significance)
Wheat requires 400 to 500 mm of water delivered across 4 to 6 irrigations of 6 to 7 cm depth:
1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI) Stage (20 to 25 Days After Sowing):**
   - ABSOLUTE HIGHEST PRIORITY. Crown roots establish the primary anchorage and root absorbing system. If this irrigation is missed, tillering is permanently stunted, causing a 35-40% irreversible yield penalty.
2. **Active Tillering Stage (40 to 45 DAS):**
   - High priority; determines the count of productive ears (spikes) per plant.
3. **Late Jointing / Booting Stage (60 to 65 DAS):**
   - High priority; determines spikelet count and potential grain number per earhead.
4. **Flowering / Anthesis Stage (80 to 85 DAS):**
   - Critical priority; moisture stress here causes pollen desiccation, sterility, and low seed set.
5. **Milking / Soft Dough Stage (100 to 105 DAS):**
   - Medium priority; grain filling and test weight. Avoid irrigating on windy days to prevent severe crop lodging.

### 2.2 Limited Water Contingency Irrigation Matrix
- **If only 1 irrigation is available:** Apply strictly at CRI stage (21 DAS).
- **If only 2 irrigations are available:** Apply at CRI (21 DAS) and Flowering (80 DAS).
- **If 3 irrigations are available:** Apply at CRI (21 DAS), Late Jointing (60 DAS), and Flowering (80 DAS).

## 3. BALANCED FERTILIZER NPK & MICRONUTRIENT SCHEDULE

### 3.1 Recommended Dose of Fertilizers
- **Timely Sown Irrigated:** 120:60:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare (approx 50:24:16 kg/acre).
- **High-Yielding Management:** 150:60:40 kg/ha.

### 3.2 Basal Placement vs Split Nitrogen Top Dressing
1. **Basal Dose (Drilled at Sowing):**
   - Apply 50% Nitrogen (Urea @ 65 kg/ha), 100% Phosphorus (DAP @ 130 kg/ha or SSP @ 375 kg/ha), and 100% Potash (MOP @ 65 kg/ha) drilled 3 to 5 cm below seed level.
2. **First Top Dressing (At First Irrigation - 21 to 25 DAS):**
   - Broadcast 25% Nitrogen (Urea @ 65 kg/ha) immediately before or 2 days after first irrigation at CRI stage.
3. **Second Top Dressing (At Second Irrigation - 40 to 45 DAS):**
   - Broadcast remaining 25% Nitrogen (Urea @ 65 kg/ha) at tillering stage.
   - CAUTION: Do not apply third nitrogen top dressing after flag leaf or boot stage; excess late nitrogen causes severe crop lodging and increases grain shattering.

### 3.3 Zinc Deficiency & Amelioration
- In North-Western plains under continuous rice-wheat cropping, zinc deficiency is widespread.
- Basal application: Apply 25 kg Zinc Sulphate heptahydrate (ZnSO4.7H2O 21%) per hectare.
- Standing crop foliar rescue: Spray 0.5% Zinc Sulphate + 2.5% Urea (5 kg ZnSO4 + 25 kg Urea in 1,000 litres of water per hectare) at tillering.

## 4. YELLOW / STRIPE RUST (*Puccinia striiformis*) SURVEILLANCE & IPM
- **Diagnostic Symptoms:** Bright yellow, powdery linear pustules arranged in parallel stripes along leaf veins; easily stains white cloth or fingers yellow upon touch.
- **Immediate Chemical Control:**
  - On first appearance of pustules in isolated focus spots, immediately spray Propiconazole 25 EC (Tilt) @ 1.0 ml/L (500 ml in 500 L water/ha) or Tebuconazole 25.9 EC @ 1.0 ml/L.
  - Repeat spray after 15 days if cool, humid, cloudy weather persists.
