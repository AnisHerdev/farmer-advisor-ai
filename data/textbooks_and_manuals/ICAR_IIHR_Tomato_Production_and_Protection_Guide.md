**Publishing Body:** ICAR - Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Hessaraghatta, Bengaluru.
**Specialized Focus:** High-Yielding Hybrid Tomato Cultivation, Fertigation Protocols & ToLCV Vector Management.

## 1. NURSERY RAISING & TRANSPLANTING METHODOLOGY

### 1.1 Protected Nursery Raising
- Damping-off (*Pythium aphanidermatum*) is the principal fungal threat in tomato nurseries during Kharif and Late Kharif.
- Solarize nursery beds using 25-micron transparent polyethylene film for 30 days during summer, or drench beds with *Trichoderma harzianum* formulation @ 50 g/m2 mixed in FYM.
- Grow nursery under 40-mesh insect-proof nylon net to exclude whiteflies (*Bemisia tabaci*), which transmit the lethal Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV).

### 1.2 Transplanting & Bed Architecture
- Age of Seedlings: Transplant healthy seedlings at 25 to 30 days old possessing 4 to 5 true leaves.
- Spacing:
  - Determinate varieties: 60 cm row-to-row x 45 cm plant-to-plant on flat beds.
  - Indeterminate staked hybrids: Raised broad beds 90 cm wide with 45 cm between rows, plant-to-plant distance 45 to 60 cm.
- Staking: Staking indeterminate plants with bamboo poles or trellis twine prevents fruit contact with damp soil, reducing ground rot and fruit borer damage by 40%.

## 2. NUTRITIONAL MANAGEMENT & FERTIGATION PROTOCOL

### 2.1 Recommended Dose of Fertilizers (RDF)
- **High-Yielding Hybrids:** 180:120:150 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare (approx 72:48:60 kg per acre).
- **Open Pollinated Cultivars:** 100:60:60 kg/ha.

### 2.2 Soil Application Schedule
1. **Basal Dose:** Apply 50% Nitrogen (Urea @ 195 kg/ha), 100% Phosphorus (Single Super Phosphate @ 750 kg/ha or DAP @ 260 kg/ha), and 50% Potash (MOP @ 125 kg/ha) along with 25 tonnes FYM and 25 kg Zinc Sulphate before bedding.
2. **First Top Dressing (25 to 30 Days After Transplanting):** Apply 25% Nitrogen (Urea @ 98 kg/ha) and 25% Potash (MOP @ 62.5 kg/ha) around plant root zones and earth up.
3. **Second Top Dressing (50 to 60 DAT - Peak Fruit Sizing):** Apply remaining 25% Nitrogen and 25% Potash.

### 2.3 Prevention of Blossom End Rot (BER) & Fruit Cracking
- **Blossom End Rot (BER):** Characterized by sunken, black, leathery patches at the blossom end of fruits. Caused by localized Calcium deficiency aggravated by uneven watering and excess ammonium nitrogen.
  - Solution: Foliar spray of Calcium Nitrate or Calcium Chloride @ 0.5% (5 g/L) twice during rapid fruit enlargement stage.
- **Fruit Cracking & Firmness:** Caused by sudden fluctuations between dry soil and heavy irrigation.
  - Solution: Foliar spray of Solubor (Boron 20%) @ 0.1% (1 g/L) at flowering and early fruit set to increase skin elasticity.

## 3. IRRIGATION & WATER MANAGEMENT

### 3.1 Water Requirements & Critical Milestones
Tomato has a shallow fibrous root system (70% active root zone within top 30 cm) and consumes 500 to 650 mm of water:
1. **Establishment Stage (1 to 7 DAT):** Immediate irrigation upon transplanting, followed by light life-saving irrigation on day 3.
2. **Flowering and Fruit Set Stage (30 to 45 DAT):** CRITICAL STAGE. Moisture stress causes flower bud drop and poor pollination.
3. **Fruit Enlargement Stage (50 to 80 DAT):** CRITICAL STAGE. Uniform moisture is essential; dry periods followed by heavy irrigation cause severe fruit skin rupture and cracking.

### 3.2 Drip Irrigation & Silver-Black Mulch
- Lay 25 to 30 micron silver-black reflective plastic mulch over drip laterals before transplanting.
- Mulch conserves 40% soil moisture, suppresses weed growth, repels whiteflies through light reflection, and prevents soil crusting.

## 4. INTEGRATED DISEASE & PEST MANAGEMENT

### 4.1 Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV) & Whitefly Vector
- **Symptoms:** Upward and downward curling of leaflets, thickened puckered leaves, extreme plant stunting, flower bud dropping, zero fruit yield.
- **Vector:** Transmitted by *Bemisia tabaci*. Once a plant is infected, systemic viral replication cannot be cured.
- **Management Protocol:**
  - Plant certified ToLCV-resistant hybrids developed by ICAR-IIHR (Arka Rakshak, Arka Samrat).
  - Install 20 to 25 yellow sticky traps per acre at crop canopy level to monitor and mass trap adult whiteflies.
  - Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (5% NSKE) or Neem Oil @ 3 ml/L as a deterrent.
  - Vector control sprays: Diafenthiuron 50 WP @ 1.0 g/L or Spiromesifen 22.9 SC @ 1.25 ml/L or Cyantraniliprole 10.26 OD @ 1.8 ml/L.
