# ICAR-CICR COTTON PRODUCTION & INTEGRATED PROTECTION TECHNOLOGY
**Publishing Body:** ICAR - Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR), Nagpur, Maharashtra.
**Authoritative Agronomic Standard:** CICR All-India Coordinated Research Project on Cotton (AICRP).

## 1. SOIL MANAGEMENT, FERTILIZATION & NUTRIENT SPLITS

### 1.1 Recommended Dose of Fertilizers (RDF)
- **Irrigated Bt Hybrid Cotton:** 120:60:60 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare (approx 48:24:24 kg per acre).
- **Rainfed Cotton:** 60:30:30 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.

### 1.2 Basal Nutrient Placement & Split Schedule
Nutrient availability must sync chronologically with cotton phenology:
1. **Basal Application (At Sowing / Final Ploughing):**
   - Apply 100% of Phosphorus (Single Super Phosphate @ 375 kg/ha or DAP @ 130 kg/ha).
   - Apply 50% of Potassium (Muriate of Potash - MOP @ 50 kg/ha).
   - Apply 20% of Nitrogen (Urea @ 52 kg/ha).
   - Incorporate 10 tonnes of well-decomposed Farm Yard Manure (FYM) or 2.5 tonnes vermicompost.
   - If soil testing reveals Zinc deficiency (< 0.6 ppm DTPA-extractable Zn), apply 25 kg Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4 21%) as basal.

2. **First Top Dressing (30 Days After Sowing - Vegetative Growth Stage):**
   - Apply 30% of total Nitrogen (Urea @ 78 kg/ha).
   - Band place fertilizer 7 to 10 cm away from plant rows and cover with soil during weeding.

3. **Second Top Dressing (60 Days After Sowing - Square Formation & Early Flowering):**
   - Apply 30% of total Nitrogen (Urea @ 78 kg/ha) along with remaining 50% of Potassium (MOP @ 50 kg/ha).
   - Potassium top dressing at this stage is vital to strengthen cell walls, prevent square shedding, and improve fiber maturity.

4. **Third Top Dressing / Foliar Nutrition (75 to 90 DAS - Peak Boll Sizing):**
   - Apply remaining 20% of Nitrogen as top dressing or deliver 2 foliar sprays of 19:19:19 (1.0%) or Potassium Nitrate (KNO3 13:0:45 @ 1.0%) at 15-day intervals to arrest boll and square shedding.
   - Foliar spray of Magnesium Sulphate (1.0%) and Boron (0.1% Solubor) prevents leaf reddening and enhances boll weight.

## 2. IRRIGATION CRITICAL STAGES & WATER CONSERVATION

### 2.1 Critical Water Sensitivity Stages in Cotton
Cotton requires 650 to 750 mm of water throughout its 150-180 day life cycle. Irrigation scheduling must prioritize phenological stages:
1. **Vegetative Stage (25 to 30 DAS):** Light irrigation only. Slight early moisture restriction forces roots to grow deep into subsoil layers.
2. **Square Formation Stage (45 to 60 DAS):** HIGH SENSITIVITY. Water stress at squaring leads to massive abscission of squares.
3. **Peak Flowering Stage (70 to 90 DAS):** CRITICAL STAGE. Water deficit impairs flower fertilization and causes flower drop.
4. **Boll Development Stage (95 to 120 DAS):** CRITICAL STAGE. Moisture stress causes premature boll opening, reduced boll size, and weak fiber microns.

### 2.2 Drip Irrigation Guidelines
- Install 16 mm inline drip laterals with drippers spaced at 60 cm delivering 2 to 4 Litres Per Hour (LPH).
- Operating pressure: 1.0 to 1.2 kg/cm2.
- Drip saves 45-50% water compared to flood irrigation and increases seed cotton yield by 20-30%.

## 3. INTEGRATED PEST MANAGEMENT (IPM) & PINK BOLLWORM CONTROL

### 3.1 Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella) Biology & Symptoms
- **Diagnostic Symptoms:** Rosetted flowers (petals webbed together with silk threads), boreholes on developing green bolls plugged with larval frass, eaten seeds, stained lint, and premature boll opening.
- **Economic Threshold Level (ETL):** 8 male moths caught per trap per night for 3 consecutive nights OR 10% infested green bolls containing live larvae.

### 3.2 Integrated Control Protocol
1. **Pheromone Trapping:** Install Gossyplure funnel pheromone traps @ 2 traps/acre for surveillance at 45 DAS; 8 to 10 traps/acre for mass trapping. Replace septa every 25 days.
2. **Biological Control:** Release egg parasitoid *Trichogramma bactrae* @ 60,000 parasitized eggs/acre at weekly intervals starting from 45 to 50 DAS (3 to 4 releases).
3. **Botanical Sprays:** Apply 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or Azadirachtin 1500 ppm @ 5 ml/litre water during squaring stage.
4. **Chemical Intervention (Only when ETL is breached):**
   - Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/L of water OR
   - Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG @ 0.5 g/L of water OR
   - Profenofos 50 EC @ 2 ml/L of water.
