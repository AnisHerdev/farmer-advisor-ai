# ICAR-CRIJAF JUTE & ALLIED FIBRES PRODUCTION MANUAL
**Publishing Body:** ICAR - Central Research Institute for Jute and Allied Fibres (CRIJAF), Barrackpore, West Bengal.
**Mandate Focus:** Agronomy, Soil Fertility, Retting Technology & Fiber Quality Standards.

---

## 1. AGRO-ECOLOGICAL REQUIREMENTS & VARIETAL SELECTION

### 1.1 Cultivar Classification & Specific Adaptations
1. **Tossa Jute (*Corchorus olitorius*):**
   - High yielding, fine golden fiber, superior tensile strength.
   - Recommended Varieties: JRO-524 (Navin), JRO-204 (Suren), JRO-8432, CO-58 (Sourav).
   - Soil Requirement: Well-drained upland alluvial soils. Highly susceptible to water stagnation during early vegetative stages.
   - Sowing Window: Mid-April to mid-May.

2. **White Jute (*Corchorus capsularis*):**
   - Tolerant to waterlogging, sub-surface flooding, and mild soil acidity.
   - Recommended Varieties: JRC-321 (Sonali), JRC-212 (Sabuj Sona), JRC-517, JRC-698.
   - Sowing Window: Mid-March to mid-April in low-lying flooded tracts.

## 2. NUTRIENT & SOIL HEALTH MANAGEMENT

### 2.1 Recommended Fertilizer Dosages
- **Tossa Jute (Olitorius):** 60:30:30 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.
- **White Jute (Capsularis):** 80:40:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.

### 2.2 Application Schedule
1. **Basal Fertilizer (Final Land Preparation):**
   - Apply 100% of Phosphorus (Single Super Phosphate @ 187 kg/ha for Tossa or 250 kg/ha for Capsularis).
   - Apply 100% of Potassium (Muriate of Potash - MOP @ 50 kg/ha for Tossa or 66 kg/ha for Capsularis).
   - Apply 25% of Nitrogen (Urea @ 32 kg/ha).
   - If soil pH is lower than 5.5 (acidic soils in Terai or Laterite belts), apply agricultural lime (CaCO3) @ 2 to 4 quintals/ha 3 weeks before sowing to neutralize aluminium toxicity.
2. **First Top Dressing (3 to 4 Weeks After Sowing):**
   - Apply 50% of Nitrogen (Urea @ 65 kg/ha) after first manual weeding and wheel hoeing when soil is moist.
3. **Second Top Dressing (6 to 7 Weeks After Sowing):**
   - Apply remaining 25% of Nitrogen (Urea @ 32 kg/ha) at the rapid elongation phase.

## 3. WATER MANAGEMENT & RETTING TECHNOLOGY

### 3.1 Water Requirements & Drainage
- Jute requires 500 to 600 mm of water with relative humidity above 70% and temperatures between 28°C and 35°C.
- Pre-sowing irrigation is essential to ensure uniform seedling emergence and prevent soil crusting.
- For Tossa jute, surface drainage is mandatory during heavy pre-monsoon showers (Kalbaishakhi).

### 3.2 Advanced Microbial Retting with "CRIJAF Sona"
- Conventional water retting requires 20 to 25 days and often degrades fiber quality when ponds lack fresh water circulation.
- **CRIJAF Sona Retting Consortium:** A dry formulation containing pectinolytic and hemicellulolytic bacterial consortium.
  - Dosage: Apply 4 kg CRIJAF Sona powder per quintal of harvested green jute plants.
  - Method: Harvest plants at 120 days (small pod stage). Defoliate in field for 3-4 days. Smear CRIJAF Sona over bundles before submergence in retting water.
  - Benefit: Reduces retting duration from 21 days to only 12-14 days, eliminates root hardness, and upgrades fiber quality by 2 full commercial grades.

## 4. PLANT PROTECTION

### 4.1 Stem Rot / Root Rot (*Macrophomina phaseolina*)
- **Symptoms:** Dark brown irregular sunken lesions near soil level on stem; affected cortex shreds into fibers revealing tiny black sclerotia.
- **Control:**
  - Seed treatment with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 2 g/kg seed.
  - Foliar spray of Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% WP (Saaf) @ 2.0 g/L upon early symptom detection.
