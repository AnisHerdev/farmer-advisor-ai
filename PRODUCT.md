# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Progressive Farmers and Common Service Center (CSC) / Village Kiosk operators across Indian agricultural belts. Users operate in rural or semi-urban environments (varied ambient daylight, intermittent connectivity, desktop/tablet kiosks) querying time-critical seasonal schedules: crop sowing windows, NPK fertilizer dosages, pest management, and irrigation stages for crops such as wheat, cotton, tomato, and jute.

## Product Purpose

An accessible, trustworthy, multilingual agricultural advisory assistant powered by localized sovereign models (`AgriParam` and `IndicTrans2`) and verified ICAR / Ministry of Agriculture knowledge bases. It exists to deliver immediate, verified agronomic answers in regional languages (Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Punjabi, and English) without overwhelming farmers with technical AI complexity or bureaucratic clutter. Success looks like a farmer or kiosk operator getting actionable, validated advice in under 10 seconds via speech or text, with transparent source verification.

## Brand Personality

- **3-Word Persona**: Grounded, Trustworthy, Simple.
- **Voice & Tone**: Calm agrarian presence, respectful, clear, and reassuring. Communicates like an experienced, supportive village agricultural officer—authoritative yet warm, without academic jargon or machine-like detachment.
- **Emotional Goals**: Reassurance, clarity, confidence, and dignity in regional language interaction.

## Anti-references

- **Cluttered SaaS / AI Engineer Dashboards**: No dark terminal aesthetics, confusing telemetry charts, hyper-parameter sliders, or LLM token streams in the primary user view.
- **Generic ChatGPT Clone**: No tiny low-contrast gray-on-gray typography, empty blank input voids, or walls of unformatted prose.
- **Dense Government Web Portals**: No labyrinthine multi-level navigation menus, intimidating PDF links, bureaucratic notices, or dense unstructured data tables.

## Design Principles

1. **Farmer-First Clarity**: Hide technical orchestration completely; present a direct, intuitive conversational surface that anyone can use immediately without prior training.
2. **Earthy Calm & High Legibility**: Employ an organic, nature-rooted aesthetic with strong typographic hierarchy that reads effortlessly on desktop monitors and kiosk screens in daylight.
3. **Transparent Trust on Demand**: Groundedness verification is readily available (via an expandable source drawer / ICAR citations) when needed, but never obstructs the direct answer.
4. **Natural Multimodal Interaction**: Treat voice and regional Indic scripts as primary citizens, ensuring seamless speech-to-text input and natural text-to-speech feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Target Compliance**: WCAG 2.1 AA standard with dedicated voice input and speech synthesis audio playback.
- **Multilingual Script Support**: Crisp typography for complex Brahmic scripts (Devanagari, Gurmukhi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada) ensuring high legibility at standard reading distances.
- **Ergonomics**: Generous click/touch targets, prominent audio controls, clear contrast ratios (≥4.5:1 for body copy), and smooth handling of reduced-motion preferences.
